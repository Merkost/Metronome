Something else on your mind?

We’re here to help
Is it really free?

Yes. All the practice features are free. No advertisements, no subscription, and no account to create.

Can I practise offline?

Yes, with the installed iOS and Android apps. Core practice works without a network connection, and your settings and practice activity are stored on your device. The browser version needs a connection to load.

Will it play with my screen off?

On iPhone, playback continues with the screen off or another app open. On Android, first enable Background Play in Settings and allow its notification permission.

Does the web app remember my practice?

The browser version currently resets settings, presets and practice sets when you reload. Install the mobile app to keep them between sessions and use background playback and haptics.

Can I change the meter and sound?

Absolutely. Choose from 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8 and 7/8, add subdivisions, and accent or mute individual beats. Wood, Click and Classic sounds are available in Settings.
