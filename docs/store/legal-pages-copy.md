# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 3 September 2026_

## Overview

Metronome is designed with your privacy in mind. Its practice features work
offline, and it does not require an account or personal information to use. The
app does send anonymous diagnostic data, described below.

## Data we collect

- **Firebase Crashlytics** (iPhone, iPad and Android) — anonymous crash reports
  to help us fix bugs, including device type, OS version and stack traces. No
  personal data is included.
- **Firebase Analytics** (Android only) — anonymous usage data such as session
  duration and screen views.
- **Firebase Performance Monitoring** (Android only) — anonymous performance
  metrics.

## Data stored on your device

Your settings, practice presets, practice sets and practice time are stored
locally on your device. This data never leaves your device.

## No personal data

We do not collect, store or share personally identifiable information. There is
no registration or login.

## No ads

The app contains no advertisements and no ad tracking.

## Third-party services

Firebase services are provided by Google. Their privacy policy is at
firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any data from children. The anonymous crash
and analytics data collected by Firebase does not include personal information
from any user.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page
with an updated date.

merkostdev+metronome@gmail.com

# Support

## Getting help

Email **merkostdev+metronome@gmail.com**. Including your device model, OS
version and the app version from Settings helps us reproduce the problem.

## Common questions

**The click sounds late or uneven.**
Close other audio apps and try a different click sound in Settings. On Bluetooth
headphones some latency is unavoidable — wired output or the device speaker is
more accurate for critical practice.

**Playback stops when I switch apps.**
Enable background playback in Settings. On Android, allow the notification
permission when prompted so the metronome can keep running.

**My practice presets disappeared.**
Presets are stored on the device and are not synced between devices or restored
from a backup of another device. If they vanished on the same device, please
email us with the app version.

**How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?**
Email the address above. Bug reports that include the steps to reproduce get
fixed fastest.
