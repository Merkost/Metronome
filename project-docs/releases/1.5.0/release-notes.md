# 1.5.0 — More space for your rhythm

A cleaner instrument, a simpler welcome and more comfortable practice controls.

## What's changed

- **One-screen welcome.** An illustrated introduction explains tempo, sound and practice with three short highlights. Get started and Skip open the instrument silently and preserve the current setup.
- **A calmer main screen.** BPM, beat state and playback have more space. The −5, +5, halve and double shortcuts now sit in Set tempo; they edit a draft until Set tempo or the keyboard's Done action confirms it. Dismissing the sheet leaves the current tempo unchanged.
- **Clearer configuration.** Rhythm uses quieter grouping and selection states. Settings uses direct Audio, Appearance, Playback, Beat display, Practice data and Help headings. Click choices are more compact, with separate selection and preview controls.
- **More focused practice.** Preset and practice-set lists, session controls, timer and trainer layouts have a simpler hierarchy. The starter routine appears when the practice-set library is empty. Opening a trainer focuses its controls without the unrelated saved-setup section.
- **Consistent motion and stable controls.** Shared motion tokens cover the refined list, disclosure and action transitions. Welcome saving feedback reserves its space, and sound selection remains independent of preview playback.

## Fixes

- Long bottom sheets now scroll the complete final action into view, with clear space above the device's bottom inset.
- Timer, trainer, welcome and other updated layouts adapt more comfortably to larger text sizes.
- Animated numeric readouts keep the correct digit order in right-to-left layouts; navigation arrows follow the surrounding layout direction.
- Android status-bar and navigation-bar icons follow the app's appearance for readable contrast.
- Unselected Mono colour choices remain visible on dark surfaces.
- Repeated welcome taps cannot trigger duplicate completion, and an existing sound selection is preserved without an unnecessary settings write.
