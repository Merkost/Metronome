# 1.6.0 — Speaks your language

Metronome in ten languages, a desktop app for the Mac and an instrument with more voice. This release also carries everything prepared for 1.5.0.

## What's changed

- **Ten languages.** Spanish, French, German, Brazilian Portuguese, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Russian and Turkish on Android, iOS, desktop and web.
- **Metronome for Mac.** Sidebar navigation, keyboard shortcuts with tooltip hints and a title bar merged into the window.
- **An instrument that talks back.** The count-in is spelled out in words, tempo names sit in clear bands, Settings sums up your practice and the timer and statistics show your week.
- **Hear before you start.** Hear one bar on the welcome step, a beat display preview in Settings and colour schemes drawn as miniature instrument faces.
- **More practice tools.** The tempo trainer can step after each gap cycle or on tap, a phrase marker accents the first bar of each phrase, and an option stops the metronome when the timer ends.
- **Steadier in the background.** The click keeps playing when the Android screen locks, Android gets lock-screen controls, and short interruptions no longer end the session.
- **A beat with character.** A strike with a tempo-aware ring, a designed dark theme and full support for reduce motion.
- **Everything from 1.5.0.** The one-screen welcome, a calmer main screen and clearer Settings.

## Fixes

- Singular forms read correctly ("1 bar", "1 step").
- Hover tints no longer stack on the ripple on desktop.
- Tempo names no longer overlap between bands.

Translations are machine-written and structurally checked; they have not been reviewed by native speakers. On desktop, iOS and web, regional variants such as Simplified Chinese with a US region fall back to English.

Each beat is a strike with a ring that follows the tempo, and the dark theme has a calmer grey ladder. The beat animation follows the system reduce motion setting.
