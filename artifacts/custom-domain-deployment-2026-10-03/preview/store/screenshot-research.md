Shipped core: 40–220 BPM with slider/steppers/quick chips and tap tempo; six time signatures (2/4–7/8); per-beat accent/mute; quarter/eighth/triplet/16th subdivisions; dots or mechanical pendulum display; Wood/Click/Classic sounds with click volume and L5–R5 stereo panning; beat haptics; colour flash; one-bar count-in; keep-screen-awake; drift-free monotonic scheduling (1.2.1); clean stops on calls/other audio. Practice tools: countdown timer (1–60 min, +5, restart) and stopwatch, today/streak/total stats, ascending/descending Tempo Trainer, Gap Trainer, and — new in 1.3.0 — Practice Presets (≤50, favourites/recents/reorder/rename/duplicate/apply, migrated from saved tempos), Practice Sets (≤30 sets × ≤20 steps with time/bar targets, previous/pause/next/finish, recovery), Practice Again, an active-practice strip, a What's New sheet, a native review prompt, and a unified calm motion system. Platform extras: Android foreground-service background playback with a Stop-only notification and Material You on Android 12+; iOS background audio plus iOS 17+ Live Activities/Dynamic Island (BPM, tempo name, meter, timer, play/pause) and native UISwitch settings. Appearance: System/Light/Dark plus five colour schemes. Free, ad-free, no account, offline core, local data (anonymous Crashlytics on both; Analytics/Performance on Android only). Not shipped and must not be claimed: web app (no web target exists despite landing copy), Android widget, long-press shortcuts, pedal/keyboard/watch control, Ableton Link/MIDI, session history/insight, polyrhythms, localization beyond English, tempo control from notification/Lock Screen. Caveats: 1.3.0 not yet git-tagged; device audio and VoiceOver/TalkBack verification pending; live store listing may still show the old name; privacy vs Play data-safety declarations unreconciled; no reviews/ratings to quote.

- Android home-screen widget — ROADMAP.md 'Next / P2A' only (extend Live Activity snapshot seam).
- Deliberate long-press shortcuts — ROADMAP.md 'Next / P2A'. (The only long-press today is onLongPressConfirm, which just plays a confirm haptic: MetronomeViewModel.kt:589-591.)
- Hardware keyboard and Bluetooth pedal shortcuts; Apple Watch / Wear OS controls; lock-screen/widget shortcuts; Ableton Link and MIDI clock sync — ROADMAP.md 'Later / P2B'.
- Private practice insight (P3): local session history, time by preset/set, weekly consistency, tempo progression, session notes, calendar history, export/delete, local reminders — ROADMAP.md 'Later'. Only daily/total time and streak exist today.
- Polyrhythms, more click sounds, per-sound accent pitch, set sharing — ROADMAP.md 'Later / Additional serious practice tools'.
- Localization: English only. ES/DE/PT/JA are in ROADMAP 'Now'; most UI text is still hardcoded English (PRODUCT.md:38; ROADMAP.md:57).
- Formal accessibility conformance: no standard selected; VoiceOver/TalkBack traversal, text scaling, reduced-motion checks remain pending (PRODUCT.md:73; ROADMAP.md:47, 56).
- Physical-device audio verification (high BPM, subdivisions, interruptions, Bluetooth, background) remains a pending release gate (ROADMAP.md:47, 53-55).
- Android notification controls are Stop-only plus tap-to-open; there is no play/pause or tempo control in the notification (androidApp/.../MetronomeService.kt:86-98). Do not describe as full media controls.
- Live Activity / Dynamic Island offers play/pause only; no tempo change from the Lock Screen (MetronomeLiveActivityWidget.swift:29-38). Requires iOS 17+; iOS deployment target is 16.0 so iOS 16 users get no Live Activity.
- Material You / dynamic colour is Android 12+ only; not on iOS (IosPlatformActions.kt:36).
- BPM range is 40–220 (MetronomeConstants.kt); CHANGELOG 1.0.0's '40 to 240' should not be repeated.
- Legacy 'Saved tempos' (max 8 bookmarks, 1.1.0) no longer have their own UI in the Tempo sheet; they are migrated into Practice Presets (no savedTempo references in TempoTrainerSheet.kt). Describe as presets, not saved tempos.
- Known stats bugs: sessions spanning midnight credit all time to the pause day; streaks do not re-evaluate at midnight while open (ROADMAP.md:103-104).
- Coach marks still reference beat circles and do not adapt to pendulum mode (ROADMAP.md:105).
- No testimonials, ratings, awards, or benchmarks exist; Apple review feeds returned zero written reviews and Play shows 1K+ downloads with no visible rating (docs/aso-strategy.md 'User Review Evidence'; PRODUCT.md:61). Do not fabricate.
- Privacy declarations are unreconciled: docs/privacy.html discloses anonymous Crashlytics (both), Analytics + Performance (Android), while the Play data-safety form says no data collected (PRODUCT.md:37; ROADMAP.md:106). Avoid strong 'no data collected' claims.

- No testimonials, awards, comparative benchmarks, or independently verified performance measurements — none are established in the repository and they must not be fabricated. (source: PRODUCT.md:61 — "No testimonials, awards, comparative benchmarks, or independently verified performance measurements are established in the repository and must not be fabricated.")
- No testimonial, rating claim, or review-derived product claim of any kind — there is no first-party review corpus (zero written Apple reviews across 12 storefronts on 2026-08-31; Google Play shows no visible rating). (source: docs/aso-strategy.md:140-145 — "There is therefore no meaningful first-party review corpus to score or quote. No testimonial, rating claim or review-derived product claim should be published yet.")
- Do not quote or rely on the stale AppBrain comment — it predates the current product and is not sufficient evidence for a marketing claim. (source: docs/aso-strategy.md:143)
- No "Featured", "Editor's Choice", award, or rating badges in frames — both stores prohibit implying editorial endorsement. (source: tools/store-screenshots/README.md:56-59 — "Do not add 'Featured', 'Editor's Choice', award or rating badges — both stores prohibit implying editorial endorsement, and docs/aso-strategy.md rules out review-derived claims until there is a real review corpus.")
- Only claims that are true of the shipped build may appear in frames.json (titles, subtitles, badges). (source: tools/store-screenshots/README.md:56 — "Only claims that are true of the shipped build belong here.")
- No unsupported superiority claims (best, #1, most accurate, etc.). (source: PRODUCT.md:48 — "Store metadata should retain immediate category recognition, communicate precision and practice value, and avoid unsupported superiority claims.")
- No stronger privacy or data-collection claims (e.g. "no tracking", "no data collected", "100% private") until the repository privacy policy, Apple privacy declaration, and Google Play Data safety declaration are reconciled. The privacy policy discloses anonymous Firebase Crashlytics (all platforms) plus Analytics and Performance Monitoring (Android), while Google Play currently declares no data collected. (source: PRODUCT.md:37 — "These declarations must be reconciled before stronger privacy or data-collection claims are published."; docs/aso-strategy.md:219 (Release Order step 1); docs/privacy.html:41-48)

- Do not let Tempo Trainer (or any single trainer) define the product; the presentation must lead with reliable timing and better practice, then prove it with the broader toolkit.
