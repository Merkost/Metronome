Quick answers

Playback stops when I lock the screen.

On Android, enable Background Play in Settings and allow notification permission so playback can run in the background. iPhone supports background playback automatically. The browser preview is best used with its tab open.

How do I use tap tempo?

Tap the Tap button repeatedly at the tempo you want. After two or more taps, the BPM adjusts to your rhythm.

How does Tempo Trainer work?

Choose a starting tempo, target tempo, step size, and how many full bars to hold between steps. The trainer changes the BPM while you play.

Can I change the click sound?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Wood, Click or Classic.

Why did my browser presets disappear?

The web version currently resets settings and saved practice on reload. Install the iOS or Android app to keep presets and practice sets between sessions.
